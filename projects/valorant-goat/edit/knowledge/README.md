# Editing and sound knowledge base

Checked 17 Nov 2026 for ay's question: is there a pro editor's Obsidian vault or repo we can plug in, so the sound
and edit aren't the bare minimum?

## Short answer

There is no public vault from a pro YouTube editor worth importing. The public Obsidian vaults are productivity
templates, and the GitHub "awesome" lists for video are lists of software. Pros don't win on a secret rulebook: our
rules in [[SOUND]] (from the video-shotcraft skill and HyperFrames) already match what the guides teach. They win
on three things we were thin on, and this folder fixes those:

1. **A better sound library**, searched and cut by Claude. See [[sources]].
2. **Music cut to the picture** (tracks ending on the beat, stripped back under key lines, custom lengths), not a
   bed that runs and fades. See [[music-editing]].
3. **Layered, consistent sound effects** with a reason on screen for each one. See [[sfx-craft]].

## The picks, best first

| # | What | Cost | Why it beats what we have |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **VALORANT Creator-Safe Playlist** (Riot, 40 tracks) | free | The game's own music, owned 100% by Riot and cleared for creators. Nothing in a stock library sounds more like Valorant. Credit line needed. |
| 2 | **Epidemic Sound + its official Claude connector** | $17.99 for one month, or $119.88 a year | Claude searches 55,000 tracks and 250,000 effects by mood, tempo or "sounds like this track", gets stems, and has the service re-edit a track to an exact length with sections pinned to times. That is the "tailored soundtrack" pros pay editors for. Videos published while subscribed stay covered after cancelling. |
| 3 | **Sonniss GameAudioGDC archive** (2015 to 2024, plus the 2026 bundle) | free | Hundreds of GB of professional game sound libraries (impacts, whooshes, UI, weapons, foley). Same royalty free licence as their paid packs, no credit needed. Far deeper than our six Pixabay files. |
| 4 | **Craft notes** in this folder | free | Concrete techniques from music editors and sound designers, written as rules our Remotion edit can follow. |

Skills and repos checked and **not** imported (we already cover them, or they add nothing on sound): see the end of
[[sources]].
