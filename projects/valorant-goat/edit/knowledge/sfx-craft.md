# Placing and layering sound effects

Adds to [[SOUND]] (which sets the cue grammar and levels). Sources at the bottom.

## Layering

1. **Stack sounds in different frequency ranges, not one sample.** A good impact is three layers: a low thump
   (under 150 Hz) for weight, a mid body for punch, a short high transient or debris for detail. A good whoosh is
   an air layer plus a low swell. Layers start within one frame of each other. The high layer matters most on
   phone speakers, where the low one disappears ([[SOUND]] rule 7).
2. **Carve a pocket for the voice.** Pull the music down a few dB in the voice band (around 1 to 4 kHz) while the
   voice plays, instead of only turning the whole bed down. Trim the voice's very low and very high ends.

## Style

3. **One family of sounds for the whole video.** Pick the set once (for us: clean digital UI ticks and tight
   tactical hits that sit next to Valorant's own sound), and use the same sound for the same job every time. A
   recognisable chapter sting is the goal.
4. **Believable and subtle beats cartoony.** "The cartoony sounds draw too much attention to themselves." No meme
   sounds, no notification bings ([[SOUND]] rule 3).
5. **Sound the feeling, not the literal action** when it helps: a stat that crushes the field can land with a heavy
   impact even though a number has no sound.

## Placement

6. **A short marker sound on any graphic that could be missed** (an arrow, a highlight, a chip landing).
7. **Risers, swells, suckbacks, impacts or slams under transitions and endings** make cuts feel joined and
   moments feel bigger. One cause on screen per sound.
8. **Study how dense the pros are.** Well-produced documentaries and ads have far more effects than you
   consciously hear. Use the reference study's measured cue counts per minute as our target, not a guess.

## Where to get layers

- Low and mid impact layers, whooshes, UI ticks: Sonniss GDC archive, Freesound CC0 (see [[sources]]).
- Valorant's own feel: credit-safe VALORANT music for beds. In-game sounds come only inside official footage we
  already show, never as separate files.
