# DRAFT TEST 3 (algorithm v3): not the final ranking

Principle (locked before running, not tuned to any winner): **GOAT = individual dominance x how much you drove winning.**

## What changed since test 2
1. **Title credit share.** Each trophy's points are multiplied by the player's own impact in that run compared with their teammates. The impact is role- and season-adjusted rating at that event, with playoff rounds counting double. Carrying a team (credit up to 1.5x) counts more than being on the roster (down to 0.5x). The team's total stays about the same.
2. **No diminishing returns** (ay's call): every trophy keeps its full value by tier (Champions > Masters > regional), times the credit share.
3. **Peak dominance.** Peak + Longevity is now 60% best rolling 12 months vs the field and 40% longevity.
4. **Two side-boards for the video:** Most Decorated (pure trophy and MVP points, no credit share) and Most Dominant (individual categories only: fragging, big games, peak, opening duels, clutch, team play, consistency).
