## The Faker move, copied from Jxmy (VCIKek1f-Hs 09:38-10:40, R4_XtNi7A08 08:34-09:14)

1. Chart opens on the pack only. The axis starts near the pack (Jxmy: wins axis starts at 290, points axis at 400), never at zero. Labels are tiny "Name (value)" under each dot; photos optional.
2. Hold on the pack about 6 s while talking ("Ruler, Bin, ... here's the top").
3. Line: "and here's Faker, by the way". Camera climbs straight up through EMPTY axis numbers, fast: Jxmy goes 300 to 400 in about 3.5 s, nothing on screen but the numbers ticking past.
4. The lone dot arrives at the top. The music cuts to silence exactly on the number.
5. Hold the dot in silence about 3 s.
6. Slow zoom out so the whole gap shows in one frame (pack at the bottom, Faker alone at the top). Optional bracket with the gap ("92 wins between 1st and 2nd").
7. Music comes back in on the next line.

Scatter version (R4 08:48-08:57): full cloud held, then a hard cut in on the top outliers with a small round photo + NAME + value, then about 3 s zoom out with labels filling down the pack. Music silent across the cut.

## Chart types to mix instead of plain bars

- Cut-axis dot climb (VCIK). Best for one huge gap.
- Dot/star scatter with circled names, colour by value red-to-green, big ellipse round the subject (R4 04:22).
- Photo-dot scatter on a flat colour (dPZ 22:06 blue, QF 13:52 dark red). Every dot is a tiny round face, one big face for the outlier.
- Grey wall of bars for everyone, one gold bar, curved arrow out to a photo callout with the stat and rank (QF 13:36).
- Race lines: everyone's cumulative line draws together, a reference line runs ahead with a photo at its tip, then everything greys out and one line at a time lights up with a photo tag (Ibc 09:10). Good for titles or trophies over time.
- Photo timeline: round photos branching off year ticks, gold arcs connecting eras (dPZ 11:10).
- Head-to-head: two cut-outs facing each other, stats tick in between, green dot on each row's winner (IVd 05:00).
- MachineLoLing style: data points are icons (champion icons = player photos for us), bell curves built by stacking icons, beeswarm per role, 5x5 heat map, red/green zone shading.

Common to all: dark background, one accent colour for the subject, everything else grey or white, no gridlines, small labels, numbers never collide with photos.

## Sound rules measured

- Jxmy cuts the music to silence on the reveal: the number (VCIK 10:12 "399 wins"), the winner's name (QF: "And for that, [name] is our 2026 ..."), or just before a board reveal (vx3 10:16, 5 s silence). Silences last 2 to 5 s, then music returns.
- He switches the track at chapters (dPZ 16:00 and 18:53 are the biggest switches measured; IVd uses black serif chapter cards).
- MachineLoLing's bed is about 7 dB quieter and nearly never cuts (one cut each, at the intro). That suits explaining; Jxmy's cuts suit reveals.
- All beds measured are soft, weak-beat instrumentals (beat strength mostly under 0.15), bass-led, about 60-95 bpm by ear-free estimate. The vocal-band number cannot prove there are no vocals; listen to the clips to confirm.
